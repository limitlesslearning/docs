## 8.1 Competency-Based Rules

Progression in Klados is governed by one unbreakable rule: you advance only when you demonstrate mastery — never by time spent, age, attendance, or any demographic factor (Section 2.4.4).

### Mastery threshold (non-negotiable for all levels)

- 90%+ overall score across all three assessment components (exam + project + community/review layer).
- No time limits or deadlines — a learner may take days, weeks, or months on a single level.
- Partial progress is saved permanently; there is no “fail and restart” penalty.
- Prerequisites from the knowledge graph (Section 5) must be 100% satisfied before any assessment for a new concept becomes available.

This directly eliminates credential inflation and knowledge gaps (Section 3.1.3) while enforcing the competency-over-credential philosophy (Section 2.4.4). Every credential issued is therefore meaningful and immediately usable by employers or institutions.

## 8.2 AI-Generated Adaptive Examinations

Every concept/level uses unique, on-demand examinations generated by AI (Ollama local or free-tier model) to guarantee fairness and anti-cheating at scale.

### Key features

- **Adaptive difficulty:** Starts at the learner’s current estimated level and adjusts in real time based on performance.
- **Question types:** Multiple-choice, short-answer, code execution (for programming), diagram annotation, simulation-based, and open-ended reasoning.
- **Uniqueness:** Each exam is generated from a seed + the learner’s past answers + current graph context. Two learners taking the same level at the same time receive completely different question sets.
- **Instant feedback + explanation:** After submission, the learner sees correct answers with references back to the exact content links (Section 6) they studied.
- **Retry policy:** Unlimited attempts with a 24-hour cooldown between full retakes to encourage genuine learning rather than guessing.

### Technical implementation (ElysiaJS + SurrealDB)

- Prompt template stored in Appendix 20.2 ensures questions stay evidence-based and ideology-neutral.
- Results are stored immutably as `assessment_attempt` records linked to the learner and concept.
- Average exam length: 15–40 minutes depending on level.

## 8.3 Project Submission & Multi-Reviewer Workflow

Practical demonstration is mandatory for every level (especially addressing the practical-skills gap in Section 3.1.8).

### Workflow

1. Learner submits a project artifact (GitHub link, uploaded file, Figma prototype, code sandbox result, etc.).
2. AI pre-scores the submission for technical completeness and plagiarism (using vector similarity against known solutions).
3. Routed to minimum 5 independent verified reviewers (Section 6.4 rules: must be at least 3 levels higher in the domain + Reviewer Rank ≥ Bronze).
4. For advanced levels (15+): minimum 10 reviewers.
5. Each reviewer gives a rubric score (0–100) + written feedback.
6. Consensus required: 80%+ agreement on final score.
7. AI acts as tie-breaker and collusion detector (Section 8.5).
8. Approved projects become part of the learner’s permanent public portfolio (with learner consent).

### Minute details

- Template gallery provides starter scaffolds for every project so beginners are never stuck.
- Version history of submissions is kept (learner can iterate).
- Reviewers earn coins + reputation for timely, high-quality feedback.

## 8.4 Interview & Defense for Advanced Levels

For levels 16+ (expert and above), a live or recorded oral defense is required to verify deep understanding.

### Format

- 15–30 minute video call (recorded for audit) with 2–3 verified experts or high-ranked reviewers.
- Learner presents their project and answers randomized follow-up questions generated by AI.
- Questions probe “why” and “what-if” scenarios to test true conceptual mastery rather than rote knowledge.
- Recording is stored immutably (with learner consent) and linked to the credential.

This mirrors real-world technical interviews and project defenses used by top employers, making the credential instantly credible (Section 4.3.4).

## 8.5 Anti-Cheating & Collusion Detection

Cheating and fraud (Section 3.1.5) are defeated through layered, multi-signal defenses:

- **AI Uniqueness:** No two exams are ever the same.
- **Behavioral Signals:** Time-per-question, mouse/keyboard patterns, and answer revision history are analyzed (privacy-preserving, aggregated only).
- **Collusion Detection:** Reviewer agreement patterns + IP/timezone clustering + graph-based anomaly detection flag suspicious review groups.
- **Security Circles:** All reviewers and learners are tied to trusted human networks (Section 10).
- **Immutable Audit Trail:** Every assessment, submission, review, and interview is hashed to the blockchain snapshot layer (Section 9.1). Tampering is cryptographically impossible.

**Consequence:** First offense → temporary suspension + mandatory re-diagnostic. Repeated offenses → permanent removal of credentials with public transparency.

The system assumes good intent but verifies ruthlessly — exactly the balance required for a global, free platform.

## 8.6 Progress Tracking & Analytics (Minute Details)

### Learner-facing

- Real-time mastery heatmap across the entire knowledge graph (color intensity = % complete).
- Personal streak counter, weekly progress report, and “Path to Next Job Profile” forecast.
- Exportable timeline PDF showing exact dates, scores, reviewers, and content used.
- Anonymous peer benchmarking (“You are in the top 12% of learners at this level globally”).

### Internal / platform analytics (governance & improvement)

- Aggregate completion rates per concept → AI suggests graph-edge adjustments.
- Reviewer performance dashboard (accuracy vs consensus).
- System-wide skill-gap heatmaps to guide future content discovery (Section 6.5).

All analytics respect privacy: learners control what is public; platform data is anonymized and used only for continuous evolution (Section 2.3.5).

### Technical storage

`progress_record` table in SurrealDB links learner → concept → score → timestamp → `blockchain_hash`. Real-time subscriptions push updates to the SvelteKit dashboard instantly.

This assessment system turns the knowledge graph (Section 5) and content (Section 6) into verifiable capability. It is rigorous enough to replace traditional degrees yet flexible and humane enough to welcome every learner on Earth.
