Klados is built in deliberate phases. The immediate focus is Phase 0 (this documentation site) and a lean Phase 1 MVP in Computer Science and Mathematics.

## Phase 0 — Documentation & Foundation (current)

Before application code, establish the structural rules and technical foundation:

- Publish and maintain the Klados Handbook as the single source of truth.
- Initialize the chosen environment (SvelteKit, Bun, ElysiaJS, SurrealDB) as documented in Section 12 (still needs drafting).
- Define the SurrealDB schema for the knowledge graph and version-control AI prompts (“Evidence-First Standard”) for content ingestion and exam generation.

**You are here:** this public docs site is Phase 0.

## Phase 1 — MVP: Computer Science + Mathematics

Do not map all human knowledge at once. MVP domains are **Computer Science** and **Mathematics** — objective fields with abundant open content and direct employer demand.

Core MVP features:

- AI-powered diagnostic onboarding (10–15 adaptive questions → Starting Graph)
- Interactive knowledge graph dashboard
- External content ingestion pipeline (YouTube / MOOCs / open PDFs; metadata only)
- Security Circles for anti-fraud
- Competency-based assessment (90%+ mastery; unique AI exams; multi-reviewer projects)
- Verifiable credentials with blockchain snapshots
- B2B Job Profiles as the revenue engine

## Phase 2 — Domain Expansion & Offline

Once MVP hiring outcomes are proven:

- Expand the graph to physics, biology, humanities, and more
- Tauri offline desktop client (cached content, local assessment subset, sync on reconnect)
- Multilingual support and regional-creator prioritization

## Phase 3 — Creator Economy & Governance

- Internal content uploads by verified creators
- Platform coins and compensation for top reviewers/creators, funded by B2B revenue
- API access for universities and enterprise partners

## Phase 4 — Global Scale & Recognition

- Sponsored practical-lab partnerships (wet labs, robotics, manufacturing)
- Official Course Profiles recognized by governments and universities
- Solidify Klados as a universal education layer bridging digital and physical learning

:::note[From notebook discussion]
Immediate focus should stay on Phase 1: graph database, AI ingestion/assessment, peer review, and corporate Job Profiles for CS + Math. Prove that a “Klados Level 15” hire can outperform traditional screening before expanding horizontally.
:::
